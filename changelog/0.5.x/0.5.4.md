# 0.5.4 — 2026-11-17

## Added

- **`award`, `awardStart`, `projects`, and `investigatorCount`** on `crossref_get_work` for a grant record, which deposits no title or abstract — project titles, descriptions, lead, co-lead, and other investigators, amounts, dates, and funding (each funder with its Funder Registry DOI or ROR). Investigators page by `offset` and `limit` across the projects in deposited order; lead and co-lead investigators come back whole on every page (#114).
- **`review`** on `crossref_get_work` for a peer-review record — stage, type, running number, revision round, recommendation, competing-interest statement, and language, where deposited (#114).
- **A `notice` on works lists carrying an untitled grant row** — `crossref_search_works` and the `crossref_search_funders` works list — naming `crossref_get_work` as the call that returns the grant's title and projects (#114).
- **`publishedVersions`** on `crossref_get_work` for a `posted-content` preprint — the works whose records name it in `has-preprint` (`doi`, `type`, `title`), at most 10, with the `notice` giving the total and the `crossref_search_works` filter that lists them all. It costs one extra request; `[]` means no Crossref record names the preprint, and the field is omitted when the lookup failed (#115).
- **`funder`, `license`, and `link`** in the `crossref_search_works` `fields` enum, returned as `funders` (at most 25 per work, with `funderCount`), `licenses`, and `links` in the entry shapes `crossref_get_work` uses. `rows` takes up to 200 when any of the three is named (#116).
- **`descendantWorksCount`** on a `crossref_search_funders` `funder_doi` lookup — the registry's work count across the funder and its descendants, less grant records (#113).

## Changed

- **Every Crossref request shares one deadline of 25 s** (`CROSSREF_TIMEOUT_MS` when longer) across its attempts, backoffs, and honored `Retry-After` waits; `CROSSREF_TIMEOUT_MS` is now the per-attempt timeout. A `Retry-After` longer than what is left fails at once as `rate_limited` with the wait in its hint.

## Fixed

- **HTML a deposit escaped into a JATS field** (`&lt;p&gt;`, `&lt;i&gt;P&lt;/i&gt;`) is read as markup and removed like deposited tags in abstracts and titles, with the references escaped alongside it decoded; a lone escaped opener that names an element stays literal, and reference text is unchanged (#111).
- **`crossref_search_funders` `worksCount`** is the `funder` filter's total less the grant records it counts, and its description says so instead of "can differ"; the `crossref-guide` skill's funder-scope guidance follows (#113).
- **A cancellation during a retry backoff** reports a cancelled request instead of the caller's raw abort reason.
- **Award numbers and license `contentVersion`** are escaped in `content[]`.
- **The `crossref-guide` skill's publisher-scope guidance** names the prefix bioRxiv and medRxiv records now carry (`10.64898`) and the case rule for `relation.object`.
